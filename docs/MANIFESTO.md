# GARY — Manifesto (skeleton)

> The firewall, in the open. Every claim wears its tier.
> This is a skeleton: headers + seed bullets, to be filled as the science lands.

## Tier 1 — SCIENCE  (measured; claims allowed; must be reproducible)

- Meaning is measurable: `I(state; signal)` in bits. Zero until a code exists.
- A "meaning" claim must survive a **null control** (shuffled state / signal → MI ≈ 0).
- Emergent communication: meaning can arise from interaction with nothing injected
  (Lewis / Skyrms). This is demonstrable, not metaphorical.
- Chaos is structured: deterministic chaos has attractors. "Disorder" is the wrong word.
- *(seed — expand with each measured result)*

## Tier 2 — OPEN  (plausible, unresolved; hypotheses only, never asserted)

- **Panspermia (weak form):** the building blocks (amino acids, nucleobases) are
  demonstrably extraterrestrial (Murchison meteorite). "Compatibilities and
  functionalities", not mission. Directed panspermia stays *open*, leaning unlikely.
- **Fermi / Dark Forest:** silence-as-equilibrium is one game-theoretic resolution among
  many (Great Filter, rare Earth, we-are-early…). Modelled here, asserted nowhere.
- **The lossy past:** records degrade; deep history is low-SNR. How much is recoverable is
  an open, bounded question — not a mystery.
- *(seed)*

## Tier 3 — INSPIRATION  (fuel only; never cited as evidence)

- The disclosure film as a **mirror** of human expectation / fear, not a document of
  contact.
- Myth, HADD (hyperactive agency detection), apophenia: studied as **objects**, never as
  beliefs. The thing we firewall is the thing we measure.
- "Chaos, but not ugly chaos." Words carry false meanings; GARY tries to give them their
  honest ones.
- *(seed)*

---

**The one rule:** a claim may only move *up* a tier by **measurement**, never by feeling.
Not needing confirmation is a feeling. The bar for belief is evidence.
